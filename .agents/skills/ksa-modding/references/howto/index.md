# Subdirectories

* [assets](assets/index.md) - The data side of a KSA mod: the asset XML vocabulary and how ids and paths resolve, custom XML element types and shared data files, glTF models and PBR textures (authoring, export, runtime import), patching and redirecting the game's own XML (KittenExtensions XPath patches, loader redirects, Core system XML), and offline and in-game validation of assets and patches.
* [audio](audio/index.md) - Playing sound in KSA from a mod through the game's FMOD system - shipping sound XML assets, placing and moving spatial channels, reusing Core sounds and their parameters, timing sounds against the world, and bringing your own files or music.
* [camera](camera/index.md) - Camera modes and their controllers, writing the pose in phase with the frame, replacing or borrowing the main view, following arbitrary points, secondary viewports and part-mounted cameras, IVA, first-person kitten views, cinematic camera animation and field of view.
* [celestials](celestials/index.md) - Celestial data and surface placement: star systems and the body tree, body data and caches, atmosphere, cloud and ring data, star catalogs and star binaries, terrain queries and modifiers, latitude and longitude picks, static objects on a planet surface and ground clutter.
* [external-io](external-io/index.md) - Everything that connects a KSA mod to the world outside the frame: sampling, exporting and recording telemetry, command queues drained on the game thread, timed scripts and AI agents, in-process HTTP/MQTT/MCP/9p/serial servers, hosted child processes, VMs and terminals, native libraries, HTTP clients and external SDKs.
* [flight-and-orbits](flight-and-orbits/index.md) - Reading a vehicle's flight state and orbit, working in KSA's coordinate frames and epochs, driving the flight computer and burns from a mod, extending the stock Transfer Planner, transfer and encounter math, targets, map-view overlays and guided flight (ascent, boostback, landing, ballistic and homing guidance).
* [getting-started](getting-started/index.md) - Build, deploy and understand a StarMap code mod end to end - project and mod.toml setup, how KSA and StarMap discover and load mods, startup hook choice, dependencies and shared assemblies, logging, version guards, fault containment and release packaging.
* [kittens](kittens/index.md) - Characters and EVA kittens - reaching the avatar, skeleton and animation catalog, the character XML, spawning kittens, per-kitten materials, animation clips and processors, posing, held props, seated crew, hiding a kitten from one view, locomotion facts and turning a kitten into a flyable vessel.
* [patching](patching/index.md) - Generic Harmony technique for KSA mods - skipping prefixes, finalizers and scoped overrides, coexisting with other mods, worker-thread patches, writing and anchoring transpilers, swapping single calls, reaching private members and patching around AggressiveInlining, and fault-contained, refusing, validated and removable patch sets.
* [persistence](persistence/index.md) - Where a mod keeps its files, how it reads and writes settings (ini, json, toml), and how it ties its own data to KSA's native saves - detecting loads and saves, sidecars, scene records, stable ids, window layouts and user data folders.
* [physics](physics/index.md) - Forces, drag and aero, wheels and suspension, colliders, Bepu, custom motion, terrain contact and physics-safe mutation of simulated state, including patches inside the worker-thread vehicle solver.
* [rendering](rendering/index.md) - Drawing things the game does not draw by itself - ImGui overlays on the sky and world, custom Vulkan passes, textures, meshes, lights, particles, plumes and decals - plus the Vulkan resource discipline that keeps those additions alive across frames, resizes and shutdown.
* [shaders](shaders/index.md) - The shader-authoring side of KSA modding - writing GLSL post-processing passes, declaring ShaderExtensions assets, feeding them parameters from C#, building common screen effects, compute effects in the HDR scene, and replacing or patching the game's own GLSL without editing game files, plus the tooling to include, compile, validate and hot-reload shaders.
* [testing-and-tooling](testing-and-tooling/index.md) - Testing and tooling for KSA mods - game-free unit tests, headless rigs and in-game HeadlessHarness suites, patch and solver tests, developer file bridges, screenshots and visual regression, unattended scenarios, live tuning and perf measurement, API-surface tracking, pinned builds and upgrade workflows.
* [ui](ui/index.md) - ImGui windows, menu bar and ModMenu entries, fonts, draw-list overlays and widgets, hotkeys and input focus, stock-window injection, HUD overlays and settings UIs for KSA mods.
* [vehicles-and-parts](vehicles-and-parts/index.md) - Defining parts and whole craft, spawning and controlling vehicles at runtime, reading and driving part modules (engines, tanks, staging, RCS, parachutes), subpart animation, scaling, crash survival, part geometry and anchors, and loading part mods into a running game.
