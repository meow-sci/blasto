# Subdirectories

* [craft-tracking](craft-tracking/index.md) - Knowing which craft matter: part-Id-driven behaviour, rosters and loss detection, and automated weapon targeting.
* [modules-and-resources](modules-and-resources/index.md) - Part modules and resources: reading live module state, refilling and draining, commanding engines and RCS, adding parts in flight and parachutes.
* [part-geometry](part-geometry/index.md) - Part-level geometry: points and meshes in vehicle, world and screen space, anchors that survive vehicle changes, and engine layout diagrams.
* [parts](parts/index.md) - Defining parts and craft in XML and code: game data, ready-made craft, seats and doors, subparts, safe evolution of shipped parts and runtime part-mod loading.
* [staging-and-engines](staging-and-engines/index.md) - Stages and engine performance: staged delta-v, thrust at ambient pressure, staging from code and automatic staging.
* [vehicles](vehicles/index.md) - Whole vehicles at runtime: spawning, safe creation and mutation, taking control, crash survival and scaling.
