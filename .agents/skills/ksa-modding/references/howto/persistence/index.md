# Subdirectories

* [saves](saves/index.md) - Tying mod data to KSA's native saves: saving and loading from code, detecting loads, sidecars, scene records, stable ids and keeping mod craft out of saves.
* [settings-formats](settings-formats/index.md) - Settings files in ini, JSON and TOML, written atomically and loaded without losing hand edits.
* [user-data](user-data/index.md) - Where a mod keeps its files and what players create: data folders, presets and content files, and ImGui window layouts.
