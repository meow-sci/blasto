# Game Feature

* [Pose a kitten's bones and aim its limbs with an IAnimProcessor](overview.md) - How a mod edits a kitten's sampled local pose or its world transforms before skinning with an IAnimProcessor, aims an arm at a point with one-bone or two-bone solves, converts between model space and the world, and draws a stepped or turned kitten without moving its body

# How-To

* [Aim an arm at a point with a one-bone shoulder turn](pose-kitten-bones-with-an-anim-processor-3.md) - KSAGolf's GolfPose turns each shoulder by the rotation that carries its shoulder-to-wrist vector onto shoulder-to-target, working in model space on the processed local pose, with no elbow solve
* [Convert a standing kitten's model space to the ecliptic or a camera's ego frame](pose-kitten-bones-with-an-anim-processor-5.md) - A KittenFrame helper repeats the engine's model-to-world chain (avatar scale, two quarter turns, minus the centre of mass, Body2Cce, vehicle position) so any model-space point converts to the ecliptic or to a given camera's ego frame without reading a drawn matrix
* [Draw a kitten stepped, turned and leaning with a stance matrix on the root bones, without moving its body](pose-kitten-bones-with-an-anim-processor-6.md) - KSAGolf multiplies a yaw-and-offset stance onto every root bone of the processed pose, leans the spine, eases the stance in on player time, refuses a step beyond 90 cm and gives the stance up when the real body drifts 15 cm, because teleporting a landed kitten makes the engine throw it about
* [Edit the local pose in UpdateLocalPose, on a simulated-time clock, with the distance cull in mind](pose-kitten-bones-with-an-anim-processor-1.md) - KSAGolf's pose probe adds a Bend IAnimProcessor to the kitten's AnimProcessors list from the after-frame hook and, once per frame, pre-multiplies rotations, adds translations and scales bones in the sampled local pose with swing, bounce and spin shapes
* [Overwrite world transforms in UpdateSkeleton once per view](pose-kitten-bones-with-an-anim-processor-2.md) - KSACars and KSAStructures write bone world matrices from IAnimProcessor.UpdateSkeleton, which runs after the clip, the processors' local edits and the world rebuild but before the skinning matrices are made, once per viewport, so a view-dependent edit is possible
* [Solve a two-bone arm in seat-relative model space with a clamped reach and an outward-and-down elbow](pose-kitten-bones-with-an-anim-processor-4.md) - KSACars reads shoulder, elbow and wrist from Skeleton.WorldTransforms in UpdateSkeleton, aims the wrist 4.5 cm short of a grip, places the elbow by the law of cosines and writes both joints with SetWorldTransform, with the maths in a KSA-free class that has tests
