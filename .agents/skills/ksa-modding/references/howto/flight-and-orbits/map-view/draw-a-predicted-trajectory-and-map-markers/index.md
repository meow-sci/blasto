# Game Feature

* [Draw a predicted trajectory and markers in the map view, and control orbit lines](overview.md) - Preview a planned burn's trajectory, multi-pass previews and impact sights on screen, draw map-view markers, arrows and overlays, and override whether a body's orbit line is drawn and recolour it

# How-To

* [Draw a flown prediction as an ImGui polyline on the background draw list, anchored to the craft and broken behind the camera](draw-a-predicted-trajectory-and-map-markers-3.md) - Store a predicted path as offsets from the craft that was flown, anchor the draw to the camera's own view of that craft, project each point to the screen and draw two stacked ImGui lines per segment on the background draw list, breaking the line wherever a point is behind the camera
* [Draw CCI and body-fixed points through a click-through full-screen overlay window with per-viewport projection and fault isolation](draw-a-predicted-trajectory-and-map-markers-5.md) - Open a transparent, input-transparent full-screen ImGui window per overlay, project inertial or body-fixed points with the main camera and drop points behind it, break polylines at gaps, guarantee ImGui.End on any exit and isolate faults so one overlay cannot take the frame down
* [Force or hide a body's orbit line and change its colour with a postfix on Astronomical.ShouldDrawLines and Orbit.OrbitLineColor](draw-a-predicted-trajectory-and-map-markers-6.md) - Override the game's decision about whether a celestial body's orbit line is drawn with a postfix on Astronomical.ShouldDrawLines driven by a mod-side Always/On/Off table, keep the body's ShowOrbit flag in step, and recolour the line by writing Orbit.OrbitLineColor while keeping alpha
* [Preview a burn's trajectory with a real FlightPlan from OrbitalTransfers.BuildFlightPlan, drawn from a TransferPlanner.OnPreRender postfix](draw-a-predicted-trajectory-and-map-markers-1.md) - Build a detached FlightPlan for a planned burn with the stock plan builder, fill its line points, add it to the frame's orbit-line instances from a postfix on TransferPlanner.OnPreRender, and suppress the stock selected-transfer preview with a paired set of prefixes that fail open
* [Preview several planned passes as dimmed orbit lines with custom markers and a quantized preview cache](draw-a-predicted-trajectory-and-map-markers-2.md) - Recolour one detached FlightPlan per planned pass with a brightness ramp, add its lines through FlightPlan.AddLineInstances, draw markers on the overlay draw list and cache the previews
* [Project orbit positions to the map view with GetPositionEclFromCce and EclToScreen and draw markers and fixed-length arrows](draw-a-predicted-trajectory-and-map-markers-4.md) - Gate a map-view overlay on the viewport's camera mode, convert a point in a parent body's CCE frame to ecliptic and to screen pixels, add the viewport position, and draw markers, a phase-angle arc and fixed-length direction arrows on the main viewport's overlay draw list
