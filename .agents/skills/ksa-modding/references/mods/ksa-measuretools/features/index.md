# Mod Feature

* [CAD-style snapping: nodes, rim centers, mirror point, vertices, edges and axial/radial components](cad-style-snap-tiers.md) - Tiered part snapping (nodes and centers, rim centers and the mirror point, then vertices and edge midpoints, then edge slide, then the raw hull hit) plus axial and radial components for rulers between parts of one vehicle
* [Circle and face-angle tools on part geometry](circle-and-face-angle-tools.md) - One click on a circular part edge yields a fitted center and rim anchor pair for diameter, radius and circumference; two hull hits yield the live angle between their part-frame normals
* [Extract feature edges and fit rim circles from a part's triangle list](mesh-feature-edges-and-circle-fitting.md) - Welds MeshReference.PositionCompare by exact position, keeps boundary, non-manifold and sharp (over 30 degrees) edges, chains them into loops and fits rim circles with a Newell normal and a Kasa fit, cached per shared mesh
* [Live anchors in body, orbit, surface and part frames](live-body-relative-anchors.md) - Each endpoint is an immutable anchor stored in its owner's frame (body CCE offset, orbit-parent offset, body-fixed lat/lon, part-local asmb) and resolved to ECL every frame, with per-frame pruning and a save-load reset
* [Measure menu and a stock-styled tool window with hover sync and click-to-copy](measure-menu-and-tool-window.md) - A postfix on the empty Program.DrawProgramMenusHook adds a Measure menu in flight and editor, opening a KSA.ImGuiWindow subclass built from ConsoleWidgets rows whose list syncs hover with the overlay and copies rows to the clipboard
* [Measuring inside the vehicle editor, including parts held in the hand](editor-measuring.md) - In the vehicle editor the tool scans the editing space and every unattached (in-hand) part tree, anchors to the editing space, lets editor drags keep their left release, and carries part measurements across editor entry and exit
* [Named color palettes persisted to a cfg file next to the mod assembly](color-palettes-persistence.md) - Overlay colors live in named palettes (four shipped plus user palettes) edited with ColorEdit4, switched from the window menu bar, and saved with a dirty flag to colors.cfg next to the DLL on close and unload
* [Own placement clicks with a guarded Program.OnMouseButton prefix](click-capture-mouse-prefix.md) - A Harmony prefix on Program.OnMouseButton takes unmodified left clicks while armed, pairs each consumed press with its release, defers to popups, ImGui, bindings and editor drags, and restores private mouse latches via reflection
* [Part-local anchors that survive staging, docking and the editor boundary](part-anchor-rehoming.md) - Part anchors keep their point in the part's asmb frame, resolve to ECL with a camera-free mirror of Vehicle.GetMatrixAsmb2Ego, and are re-homed by finding the same Part object in the editor trees or any vehicle
* [Per-patch Harmony application, drift checks, once-only logging and debug perf scopes](resilient-patching-and-diagnostics.md) - The entry class applies each Harmony patch class separately, type-checks private reflection targets at load, logs each per-frame exception type once, and in DEBUG builds times hot paths with Stopwatch scopes
* [Pick hull points, attach nodes and part centers on vehicles under the cursor](part-mesh-raycast-picking.md) - Gates vehicles by projected size and bounding sphere, raycasts parts with the stock Part.RayCastEgo over each MeshViewModule triangle list, and scans attach nodes and part centers in screen space, optionally reusing the stock hover
* [Shared MSBuild deploy targets, release zip and SpaceDock publishing](mod-deploy-targets-and-release.md) - An imported ModDeploy.targets deploys the DLL and mod.toml with a locked-file check that names the running game, a generated StarMap launch profile and a deploy stamp; a Release zip is published to SpaceDock by a workflow
* [Snap to any visible orbit line with the stock nearest-orbit-point machinery](orbit-line-snapping.md) - Runs the stock Orbit.GetNearestPosition and OrbitHoverCandidate.Wins over the stock burn picker's candidate set for every body, re-checks winners on screen to close a behind-camera NaN hole, and anchors to the orbit's parent
* [Snap to bodies and to the edge of their projected disc in the map view](body-and-limb-snapping.md) - One scan projects every body's center and disc radius, snaps inside a disc to the smallest body, snaps the disc-edge band to the limb point toward the cursor, and gates dot-sized bodies on the game's hover-eligibility flag
* [Surface tool with body-fixed pins, great-circle distance and an occlusion-aware arc](surface-great-circle-tool.md) - Ray-casts the cursor against each celestial's mean-radius sphere, pins hits as body-fixed lat/lon, reports haversine distance, chord and bearing, and draws the great-circle arc while hiding samples on the far hemisphere
* [Unproject the cursor through the view camera onto a construction plane](cursor-unprojection-and-construction-plane.md) - Free points come from Camera.ScreenToEgoRay intersected with a camera-facing or ecliptic plane through the previous point or a reference body, relying on ego axes being ECL axes and on the ray origin for the editor's orthographic projection
* [World-space measurement overlay on the ImGui background draw list](overlay-drawlist-rendering.md) - Draws measurements, previews, snap markers and plated labels into the main viewport's background draw list from StarMapAfterGui, culls behind-camera points, and throttles the hover pick to 45 ms with a state-version check
