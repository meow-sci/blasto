# Subdirectories

* [compute-effects](compute-effects/index.md) - Compute-shader effects in the HDR scene: packing parameters, reconstructing positions from depth and raymarching local volumes.
* [glsl-workflow](glsl-workflow/index.md) - Writing and maintaining mod GLSL: including the game's shader library, compiling at runtime, hot reload in a running game and offline compile checks.
* [post-processing](post-processing/index.md) - ShaderExtensions post-processing passes: the fragment-shader contract, XML assets, pre- versus post-ImGui placement, uniform buffers and push constants.
* [screen-effects](screen-effects/index.md) - Ready-made full-screen effects: colour grading, blur, grain and dithering, CRT curvature on one ImGui window, refraction and view shake.
* [stock-shaders](stock-shaders/index.md) - Changing the game's own shaders: redirected and text-patched copies, in-memory patching at compile time, smuggled data, per-body branches and sky and cloud extensions.
