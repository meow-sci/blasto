# Game Feature

# How-To

* [Spawn a one-part vehicle built in code](spawn-a-vehicle-at-runtime-3.md) - Create a Vehicle from a single PartTemplate with new Part and CreateOwnTree, with no craft file, and set it on the ground
* [Spawn a parked copy of a library craft at a latitude and longitude](spawn-a-vehicle-at-runtime-1.md) - Load a craft from the stock or player vehicle library, build it on a throw-away orbit under the shapes lock, attach it to its parent body and teleport it onto the ground
* [Spawn test drones into orbit from a state vector](spawn-a-vehicle-at-runtime-2.md) - Build a vehicle on an orbit created from a computed ecliptic state, choose Vehicle or KittenEva from the save, and attach it under the shapes lock
