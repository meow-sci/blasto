# Submod

* [Blinky](blinky.md) - Builds NxM grids of real engine parts on a live vessel at runtime and lights them as an LCD display by switching engine controllers, with feed wiring, patterns and scrolling text
* [Bloomin' Onion](bloomin-onion.md) - Defines brand-new planetary rings at runtime for any celestial body, with painted band textures, dust and rock-field settings, presets, and a renderer rebuild through the game's own settings path
* [BYO Music](byo-music.md) - Attaches imported OGG, WAV or MP3 files to vessels as 3D sounds on KSA's own FMOD system, with seamless loops, real-time gaps between plays, live edits and scene saves
* [Camera Controller Override](camera-controller-override.md) - Cinematic camera animations and keyframe sequences that pre-empt the stock Orbit and Fly camera controllers while a sequence plays, with eased target-relative moves and saved recipes
* [Dent Wizard](dent-wizard.md) - Fires an existing vessel, EVA kitten or debris fragment from the camera at a clicked vessel or terrain point with target-relative speed, launching at the shared physics handoff
* [DOH (Dynamically Originating Hominids)](doh.md) - Spawns EVA kittens on demand next to any vehicle, optionally with per-kitten tints made by cloning GPU materials inside the game's fixed 512-slot material pool
* [Don't Stifle Me](dont-stifle-me.md) - Vehicle-editor un-limiter: lifts the 0.5x-2x part scale clamp, restores per-axis gizmo scaling with optional unsnapped values, and can widen parachute diameter to 2-1000 m with exact restore of authored bounds
* [Eternal Flame](eternal-flame.md) - Keeps monitored vessels topped up with fuel and battery charge from a solver-dispatch prefix that runs before the vehicle worker snapshots module state
* [fixme-mod-name (template)](fixme-mod-name.md) - The repository's template mod pair, copied and renamed by mkmod.ts to start a new feature, showing the minimal StarMap host, Harmony patcher with hotkey guard and sibling library project
* [Free Fallin](free-fallin.md) - Applies one global look to every parachute canopy by swapping a runtime material into each canopy renderable as it draws, with tints, PBR controls, composited or projected PNG images and exact stock restore
* [Garry's Torch](garrys-torch.md) - Welds one vessel to a part of another by re-teleporting it every physics handoff, with per-axis part scaling, scoped collision suppression, eased animations, presets and a public API
* [Glass](glass.md) - Overrides the main camera field of view with lens presets from 15 degree telephoto to 120 degree fisheye, kept in place by two main-camera-only Harmony prefixes
* [Godzilla](godzilla.md) - Resizes vessels and EVA kittens at runtime with Smart or Basic scaling and independent visual, collider and physics channels, all applied at the physics handoff and exactly restorable
* [Graffiti](graffiti.md) - Click-to-place projected PNG decals on vehicle hulls, deployed parachute canopies, EVA kittens and terrain, rendered in a custom Vulkan pass after the main view's final resolve
* [Hot Pursuit](hot-pursuit.md) - Mounts live cameras on clicked vessel parts by leasing the stock secondary viewports and replacing the fixed camera controller only for those viewports, with mesh-precise placement and stable part anchoring
* [Humble Arteest](humble-arteest.md) - Three recoloring tools on three render paths, per-part vehicle paint in free state-flag bits with an in-memory shader patch, kitten material tints and visor toggle, and engine heat-glow overrides
* [I Feel Seen](i-feel-seen.md) - Keeps selected vessels and EVA kittens rendered at any distance by bypassing the game's one-pixel cull with skip-original prefixes on two Vehicle render methods
* [Iron Man](iron-man.md) - Lets one live EVA kitten be edited in the stock vehicle editor with private attachment nodes, then flown as an ordinary rocket vessel with headward controls, native flight-computer gauges, early equipment rendering and upright surface teleports
* [It's So Shiny](its-so-shiny.md) - Builds Blinky-style pixel grids from stock light parts instead of engines, powered through battery connections and switched with the stock light switch, with pattern, static and scroll controls
* [jplrepo](jplrepo.md) - A tiny development tribute mod that animates cheer phrases on KSA's top menu bar using a call-site transpiler on Program.DrawMenuBar and a prefix on DrawProgramMenusHook
* [Kitchen Sink](kitchen-sink.md) - A grab bag of small KSA fixes: per-vessel G-load invincibility, a free-flying IVA camera, a see-into-the-capsule rendering experiment, forced IVA interior rendering and an editor refresh for invisible sub-parts
* [Kitten Animations](kitten-animations.md) - Plays any loaded animation clip on a selected EVA kitten against the game's per-frame clip choice, triggers facial expressions through a mod-owned processor, and tunes animation strength and locomotion animation settings
* [Kiwi's Marbles](kiwis-marbles.md) - Welds planets and moons to other celestials or vessels at a CCI-frame offset by rewriting their orbits in a solver-dispatch prefix, with explicit parent-tree fix-ups, weld chains and scene-save restore
* [Parts Now](parts-now.md) - Loads, reloads and unloads Part and SubPart mods in a running game, from pasted XML or an existing mod folder, by replaying KSA's boot asset pipeline one state per frame behind validation, a reflection self-test and a fail-closed purge
* [Pebbles](pebbles.md) - Replaces the meshes, materials, scale and colliders of chosen ground clutter types on one celestial with stock or imported GLB models, authored in a floating collider workshop and committed at the solver handoff with exact restore
* [Pyro](pyro.md) - Places engine-free volumetric exhaust plumes on any vessel part by submitting mod-owned exhaust instances into the game's own plume batch, with nozzle physics, per-plume look overrides, a template editor, presets and on/off cycles
* [Rocky McRock Face](rocky-mcrock-face.md) - Swaps the meshes, textures and rock-field settings of existing planetary rings at runtime by mutating the ring reference tree and rebuilding the ring renderer, with a catalog of every loaded mesh and texture
* [Save Yourself](save-yourself.md) - Opt-in timed auto saves that write ordinary native KSA saves through the game's own save path every N wall-clock seconds, with a sanitised name prefix and a global TOML preference file
* [Skittles](skittles.md) - Global ImGui theme manager that re-themes every game and mod window through ImGui.GetStyle(), with built-in schemes, a live style editor, TOML theme files and scene-saved styles
* [Sphinx](sphinx.md) - Places imported textured GLBs as body-fixed statics with automatic box or mesh colliders, live transforms and UV mapping, drawn through the game's static-object pipelines from three render postfixes and restored by scene saves
* [The Tick](the-tick.md) - Makes chosen vessels indestructible by forces with a crash-tolerance getter prefix and a worker-thread discard of structural destruction events, leaving shared part templates untouched
* [Thug Life](thug-life.md) - Draws the pixel-art sunglasses meme as a textured quad anchored to any vessel part or kitten face, recorded with stock unlit shaders inside the scene's opaque pass from a postfix on SuperMeshRenderSystem.RenderMainPass
* [unscience (supermod host)](unscience.md) - The only shipped mod: one StarMap entry class that constructs all 31 bundled feature sub-mods, applies their patches through one Harmony instance, renders them in one toolbox window and adds a per-save scene sidecar
* [Zippo](zippo.md) - Selects vessels and light parts, writes light intensity, color and switch state, queues eased transitions and runs Disco party-light recipes on private per-instance light templates, with a public API and scene-save restore
