# Mod Feature

* [A ballistic computer that flies any player-built rocket to a latitude and longitude](icbm-ballistic-computer.md) - A per-craft computer samples the vehicle in the parent body's inertial frame, solves universal-variable Lambert over flight time against a spinning-planet target, steers by velocity-to-be-gained, times cutoff to the frame boundary and checks itself against a flown impact prediction
* [A burst's whiteout and a blast front's view shake, painted into the scene image instead of moving the camera](burst-flash-and-view-shake.md) - A nuclear flash whites out the view on an eye-adaptation model and a passing blast front shakes it, both stepped on the simulated clock and written by compute dispatches into the HDR scene before bloom, never into a camera
* [A generated include that reaches KSA's own shader library from a mod shader](core-shader-library-include.md) - At StarMapImmediateLoad the mod writes Shaders/Content/CoreAtmosphere.glsl with absolute includes of the install's Global, AtmosphereLuts and TextureSet headers, so its compute shaders compile against KSA's atmosphere code without copying it, and a glslang script checks them offline the same way
* [A MIRV post-boost bus that trims with RCS, re-points between releases and lets its warheads go](mirv-bus-trim-and-release.md) - After cutoff the bus nulls its velocity error against the propagated burnout solution one translation axis at a time in pulse mode, waits out the split, re-points so each tube lies on the release line, releases on a cost-of-holding rule, then goes quiet so KSA can put it back on rails
* [A mod compute pass recorded into KSA's frame just before bloom](compute-pass-before-bloom.md) - One Harmony prefix on the public SunbloomRenderer.Render hands the mod KSA's command buffer while the HDR scene colour is in a storage layout and depth is sampleable, and the mod dispatches its own Shader-asset compute pipelines there through KSA's ComputePipelineWrapper
* [A raymarched mushroom cloud, held body-fixed and drawn before bloom](raymarched-mushroom-cloud.md) - Each nuclear cloud is held in its planet's rotating frame and aged on simulated time, its Glasstone-sized shape packed into a 128-byte push constant, then raymarched per pixel into a layer that a temporal resolve blends into the scene before bloom
* [A three-member, text-only registration API for code packs, with a generated contract record](armoury-pack-registration-api.md) - KSArmory exposes Armoury.Register(string, string) so a dependent StarMap mod can add weapons from its entry point using only KSArmory.dll, returns faults instead of throwing, closes the door at StarMapAllModsLoaded, and guards the contract with a generated surface file that CI diffs
* [A weapon system is data plus art, discovered by part Id](weapon-registry-and-profiles.md) - Launchers, rounds, sensors, optics and craft components are plain profile objects in a registry that is open until all mods load and then frozen, and the mod crews one weapon system per matching launcher part it finds on any craft, following that part across splits and docks
* [Analytic sub-frame fuses by closest approach](analytic-subframe-fuses.md) - Proximity, contact, timed, height and ground fuses are solved in closed form inside each integration sub-step, so a round at kilometres per second cannot step over its trigger radius and the burst instant is known to a fraction of the step
* [Articulated turrets by rewriting subpart transforms every frame](articulated-mount-subparts.md) - A single editor part traverses, elevates, recoils and spins its barrels because the mod finds named subparts at runtime and writes their parent-relative position, rotation and scale from rate-limited drives each frame
* [Asset-only weapon packs read from a KSArmory folder in every installed mod](weapon-packs-from-mod-folders.md) - At StarMapAllModsLoaded KSArmory walks KSA's mod manifest, reads every XML file in a KSArmory/ folder inside each mod (its own included), and registers weapon definitions through a strict, culture-invariant reader that refuses unknown attributes by name
* [Ballistic lead for unguided shells, from a fixed-point solve to flying the shell](unguided-ballistic-lead.md) - A gun is laid by iterating time of flight to a tolerance against the target's relative motion and acceleration, then refined by flying simulated shells through drag, gravity and a turning ground with a Broyden-updated aim correction, with a halving search for targets beyond reach
* [Blast damage per part against the engine's crash tolerance, with health and engine dents](blast-damage-and-part-health.md) - A burst judges every part of every craft on its own distance and the engine-derived Part.CrashTolerancePascals, charges a persistent per-part health ledger, queues the parts that break, and hands the loads short of breaking to KSA's own dent system when the blast front arrives
* [Borrow the player's main view and hand it back](borrow-main-view-camera.md) - Mod camera owners borrow KSA's main view by recording its follow target, mode and field first, ranking claims with a pure ViewClaim ladder, standing down when the player changes either half, and restoring the halves separately
* [Chase camera riding a mod-simulated round](chase-camera-on-a-round.md) - The main view follows a round through a custom IFollowable that reports the round's drawn position, on a reflection-installed FixedController subclass that adds an up vector and re-solves the pose inside the engine's viewport pass
* [Closest-point-of-approach threat classification](cpa-threat-classification.md) - Contacts are classed as threats by where straight-line relative motion takes them within a horizon, not by closing speed, then ranked by time to closest approach, with a closing-only rule for munitions and a median-filtered acceleration kept beside each track
* [Command vehicles from a Vehicle.PrepareWorker prefix](prepareworker-prefix-command-window.md) - Every write KSArmory makes to another craft (attitude, pulse mode, throttle, staging, blast impulse, tanks, store mass) is queued and performed in a Harmony prefix on Vehicle.PrepareWorker, the only point where it survives KSA's double-buffered vehicle state
* [Compare mod variants in flight with interleaved batches and rank statistics](shot-night-statistics.md) - A batch harness flies unattended ballistic shots of every prebuilt variant in seeded interleaved order with the deployed DLL hashed per shot, and a report judges arms with an exact Wilcoxon rank-sum test, Hodges-Lehmann effects, a stopping rule and within-run splits
* [Cursor-ray picking and world click tools](cursor-ray-world-click-tools.md) - The ImGui cursor becomes an Ecl ray through the viewport under it (Camera.ScreenToEgoRay), hit-tested against hulls (Part.RayCastEgo) and terrain, driving fire-at-spot, shift-click lock and test-burst tools guarded by WantCaptureMouse and still run when the UI is hidden
* [Deploy into the KSA mods folder, package a clean archive and cut releases from commits](build-deploy-package-release.md) - Scripts that build and install the mod with its manifest.toml registration and a developer marker, package an archive that refuses any DLL but the mod's own, and a semantic-release pipeline that drafts a GitHub release, attaches the archive and republishes it to SpaceDock
* [Designate a place on the ground that moves with the planet, and show what a falling store can still reach](ground-target-designation-and-reach.md) - A click on terrain becomes a Ground aimpoint stored in the body's own fixed frame and re-read into ecliptic position and velocity (orbit plus spin) every frame, and a falling guided store's remaining reach is flown and drawn so a click is either a correction or the next aim
* [Destroy vehicles and break off parts safely from a frame hook](safe-vehicle-destruction-and-part-failure.md) - Kills are batched behind one JobSystems.VehicleSolver.Wait barrier, part losses are queued into the vehicle's private update state as a PartFailureEvent that the engine applies with its own crash damage, disposal uses DestroyVehicle under a shapes lock, and debris is recognised by KSA's split naming
* [Detect a save load with a postfix on Program.OnGameLoaded](detect-save-load-ongameloaded.md) - A one-line Harmony postfix on the public static Program.OnGameLoaded latches a flag that the next simulation step consumes to drop every round, system, effect and clock boundary belonging to the world the save replaced
* [Director camera windows on leased secondary viewports](director-camera-window.md) - An optical director leases one of KSA's four spare viewports, aims its unfollowed Fixed camera with LookAt from the GUI hook, hides KSA's own window by reflection and shows the viewport texture in a mod ImGui window with pod controls
* [Drive a running game from outside through a file command bridge and an MCP server](file-command-bridge-and-mcp.md) - A developer-only bridge polls a folder beside the mod log for JSON commands (pause, step, burst, camera, capture, shader reload, spawn, fly), answers each atomically in a second folder, and a stdlib Python MCP server turns that folder into tools that return captures inline to an agent
* [File bug reports from an ImGui form through a guarded issue service](in-game-bug-reports.md) - An ImGui report window posts a summary, details and the tail of the mod's own log to a small self-hosted service that files a GitHub issue, sending from a background task only when the player clicks Send and only when the running KSA build matches the one the mod was built for
* [Fire control ladder and per-craft target allocation](fire-control-and-target-allocation.md) - Each weapon system decides once per simulated step whether to launch through an ordered ladder of named gates (platform, magazine, lock, drives, IFF, gimbal, salvo, envelope) that also reports why it holds, counting rounds per target per craft from what is in the air
* [Fireball light through LightDebug, filled in where the light pre-pass dropped it](fireball-light-injection.md) - A nuclear fireball's point light is pushed into KSA's light system through the public LightDebug list, and a compute dispatch before bloom adds its diffuse term on craft and structures the clustered pre-pass culled beyond about 3 km, found by reading the pre-pass irradiance image by reflection
* [Flare and chaff dispenser parts with simulated decoys](countermeasure-dispensers.md) - Radial ALE-47 dispenser parts throw mod-simulated flares and chaff in timed salvos, by button or missile warning, and each decoy flies as a dragged point with a fading signature that feeds the radar's chaff notch and seekers that judge each decoy once
* [Fly any vehicle from a mod through its flight computer and input actions](fly-another-vehicle.md) - Another craft is flown through the flight computer's Custom attitude target, keyboard actions sent with Vehicle.ProcessInput and its staging sequence, with attitude and staging written from the PrepareWorker prefix so the worker snapshot sees them, then handed back
* [Fly scripted scenarios unattended and report a pass or fail verdict](unattended-scenario-runner.md) - A developer-only state machine reads a request file beside the mod log at load, loads a save, spawns targets, flies an engagement, bomb drop, gunnery run or ballistic shot, and writes tagged SCENARIO lines that a shell harness waits on, screenshots and turns into an exit code
* [Guided bomb tail kit and a flown bomb sight](guided-bomb-tail-kit-and-sight.md) - A falling store steers by predicting its own landing and pushing square to its airflow to move that landing onto a ground designation, while the bomb sight flies a throwaway copy of the same round in a ground-carried frame and draws the arc and impact ring
* [Gun tracers added into the scene by one tiled compute dispatch](compute-shell-tracers.md) - Gun rounds are projected on the CPU into screen-space segments in a host-visible storage buffer when the pass records, and one compute dispatch gathers them per 16x16 tile and adds depth-tested tracer streaks before bloom, with gizmo lines as the fallback
* [Gun travel limits swept from the craft's own geometry with part ray casts](self-collision-travel-map.md) - Each gun mount's traverse arc and per-bearing elevation band are discovered by ray-casting its barrel and line of fire against the other parts of its craft, on a budget of 50 rays and 1 ms a frame once the craft has held its shape for 60 frames
* [Gunner's sight painted over a borrowed view or a camera window](gunner-sight-overlay.md) - The gunner's sight is drawn with ImGui draw-list strokes over whichever view a director drives, picking the background, platform-viewport foreground or window list per surface, projecting through that view's camera and zooming by magnification
* [Headless Blender model generation and offline GLB checks](blender-model-pipeline-and-checks.md) - Three parts are generated by a headless Blender script into one palette-textured GLB atlas, and Python checkers that need neither Blender nor the game catch z-fighting, zero-area UVs, unpaired node names, swept collisions and launch geometry that has drifted from the C# profiles
* [Headless tests enforced by a game-free Sim/ source boundary](sim-boundary-headless-tests.md) - All simulation code lives in a folder that may not reference KSA types, and the xUnit project links that folder wholesale against only the numerics assemblies, so the boundary enforces itself and whole engagements are flown with no game present
* [Hold timewarp down while rounds or burns are in flight](timewarp-policy-during-engagements.md) - A closed-loop policy that reads the step KSA applied, writes Universe.SetSimulationSpeed down to the speed whose step a round can integrate, waits out the two-frame actuator delay, yields to the player or auto-warp, and gives the speed back when the air is clear
* [Holed tanks that drain through the engine's mole states and throw a painted stream](holed-tank-leaks.md) - A shell hole under a tank's liquid level drains it at an orifice rate through Mole.ConsumeStored in a Vehicle.PrepareWorker prefix, with the bookkeeping KSA's DepleteConsumables does, and big leaks throw a stream painted before bloom
* [IFF teams, alliances and the per-frame team roster](iff-teams-and-allegiance.md) - KSA has no sides, so the mod keeps its own team names, puts a craft on a team through the flag on its panel row, rebuilds a reference-keyed craft-to-team roster every simulated step, and classifies every contact as friendly, hostile, neutral or unknown against per-team rules of engagement
* [Integrate on the step KSA applied, consumed once](consume-applied-sim-step.md) - The mod advances its own simulation by Universe.GetLastSimStep, deduplicated on the step's NextTime and widened to the span since the last step it integrated, never by the player-time delta StarMap passes
* [JIT-compile the mod and the engine's destroy path on a background thread at load](jit-warmup-kill-path.md) - At StarMapAllModsLoaded a Task.Run walks every method of the mod assembly and six engine types and calls RuntimeHelpers.PrepareMethod, so the first kill of a session no longer pays tens of milliseconds of JIT in one frame
* [Launcher parts get lighter as their stores leave](per-part-store-mass.md) - Each frame the mod works out how much a launcher or dispenser part has shed in fired rounds, then rescales that part's inert mass through KSA's per-part InertMass slot and recomputes the craft's mass properties inside a Harmony prefix on Vehicle.PrepareWorker, before the physics worker copies them
* [Mod-authored particle emitters taken from KSA's shared pool and re-anchored each frame](pooled-particle-emitters.md) - Plumes, muzzle flashes, flares, chaff, flak smoke and blast dust are the mod's own ParticleEmitter assets taken by Id from the engine's fixed pool, anchored to a celestial's rotating frame, re-pointed each frame while following, and always Kill()ed before return
* [Mod-simulated rounds integrated in sub-steps inside the frame](mod-simulated-rounds.md) - Missiles, shells and bombs are not KSA vehicles but plain C# objects the mod integrates itself, in fixed sub-steps across each applied simulation step, from the same pass that draws them
* [Motor and flare smoke through KSA's volumetric booster-trail renderer](trail-renderer-smoke.md) - Burning rounds and flares lay billowing body-fixed smoke by submitting one PlumeTrailEmitterState cursor each per frame to the engine's own VolumetricTrailRenderer, reached through one reflected private field, with each segment thrown back along the trail so it swells
* [One check script shared by the pre-push hook and CI](repo-static-checks.md) - One check-all script runs about twenty textual and offline checks (Sim/ boundary, network egress, planet-agnostic code, XML, settings reachability, shaders, documented numbers, tracked artefacts) from a git pre-push hook and from CI, skipping game-dependent ones with a notice
* [One ecliptic frame and one epoch for everything simulated and drawn](ecliptic-frames-and-epochs.md) - All mod state lives in KSA's ecliptic frame at Universe.GetElapsedTime(), targets are back-dated to the round's sub-step instant, and drawing goes through the engine's own Camera.GetPositionEgo so overlays and bursts land where the craft is rendered
* [One simulation step per frame from whichever hook runs first](dual-hook-simulation-step.md) - A frame latch hands the mod's step to the StarMap GUI postfix when the HUD is drawn, to a by-name Harmony prefix on Program.OnFrameCelestials when F2 or a screenshot hides it, and to the OnFrame postfix as a last resort
* [Operator panel in ImGui with a menu-bar entry and ModMenu registration](operator-panel-imgui.md) - A multi-window Dear ImGui operator panel (craft switcher with drawn icon buttons, tabbed per-craft window, weapons switcher, pop-out panes, reopen button) drawn from the after-GUI hook, plus a menu from the before-GUI hook and a copied attribute that lets ModMenu list the mod
* [Optical director heads with mast and roll-nod gimbals](optical-director-heads.md) - Optical directors are inert parts whose head and pod roll collar are subparts slewed by a rate-limited drive, clamped to a mast elevation band or a roll-nod annulus with a keyhole, and resolved into an eye, sight line and roll for camera and laser
* [Own log file, build stamps and a developer marker file](own-log-file-and-developer-switch.md) - A batched, thread-safe log written to KSArmory.log beside KSA's logs with the previous session kept as KSArmory.prev.log, a version and KSA-build stamp read from assembly metadata, and developer tools switched on by an empty file named developer next to the DLL
* [Per-craft settings as a JSON sidecar inside the save folder](per-craft-settings-json-sidecar.md) - Each weapon system's switches are flattened into a versioned-by-defaults DTO and kept in saves/<save>/KSArmory/systems.json keyed by craft name, read when a system is crewed and written only when universe.xml's timestamp shows the game saved
* [Per-triangle hull hit test with the engine's part ray cast](hull-triangle-hit-test.md) - A shell's step is tested against the target craft's real part meshes with KSA's own watertight Part.RayCastEgo, cast in a metres-scale frame centred on the round, after a bounding-sphere broad phase
* [Pin the KSA build with a hashed assemblies lock file](ksa-assemblies-lock.md) - A committed lock file of SHA-256 digests per referenced game DLL keeps local builds, CI's private assemblies mirror and the running game on one KSA build, and stamps that build into the mod for a runtime comparison
* [Point gravity, body fall and one-reference air for mod-integrated bodies](point-gravity-and-atmosphere-model.md) - Rounds the mod integrates itself feel mu over r squared toward their body back-dated to the sub-step, plus the body's own fall toward its primary, and are dragged by air and water read from KSA's atmosphere and ocean against one fixed reference density, avoiding KSA's IsValid traps
* [Push a craft with a blast wind by writing an impulse into its physics state](blast-shove-impulse.md) - Each part the blast front reaches adds a drag-style push and a torque about the centre of mass, saturated so nothing outruns the wind, and the summed impulse is written into the vehicle's kinematic state from the PrepareWorker prefix the way Vehicle.Split does it
* [Radar PPI scope and shaded-relief terrain map drawn with ImGui primitives](radar-scope-and-relief-map.md) - A craft-centred polar radar scope and a 64x64 hill-shaded relief map are drawn only with window draw-list primitives in a north-up frame from the body's rotation axis, the heights resampled from the engine's height field only on movement, span change or every ten seconds
* [Radar search cone, masking and lock dwell](radar-search-and-lock.md) - A per-weapon radar rebuilt every simulated step from a once-per-pass vehicle census plus the mod's own rounds, filtered by range, cone, Doppler, clutter, planet, terrain, chaff and fireball, and held to a dwell time before a track becomes a firing solution
* [Record the exact KSA API a mod binds to from its compiled metadata](api-surface-tracking.md) - A small System.Reflection.Metadata tool lists every KSA type and member the built mod references, committed as a generated document, checked in CI and used to narrow a KSA update's decompiled diff to what can actually break
* [Repair saves at startup after removing a part's subparts](startup-save-repair.md) - Before any save can be loaded, the mod rewrites every save and library craft that lists more subparts for one of its parts than the part now declares, because KSA pairs saved subparts with the definition by position and a surplus kills the process on load
* [Round bodies drawn past the engine's pixel cull and as part-model instances](round-body-draw-hooks.md) - A postfix on Vehicle.UpdateRenderData redraws a sub-pixel launcher while its rounds fly, and a prefix on PartModelRenderer.UpdateRenderData adds gun shells and the rounds of a destroyed launcher as extra instances of the engine's own part meshes, per viewport
* [Rounds drawn as hidden launcher subparts flown by transform writes](rounds-as-hidden-launcher-subparts.md) - Each tube's missile is a pre-declared subpart of the launcher, seated in its tube or shrunk to a millimetre while unfired, then placed every rendered frame at the mod-simulated round's position and carried attitude by writing its parent-relative transform
* [Seekers, command uplink and laser designation](seekers-uplink-and-laser-homing.md) - Guided rounds are broken by what they can see, so the mod models a seeker gimbal, decoy seduction judged once per decoy, a command uplink that dies with the launcher's track, anti-radiation homing with emission memory, and laser spots cast on meshes and terrain (not yet homed on)
* [Separate a launcher from its stack by firing the decoupler on its own joint](launcher-separation-at-decoupler.md) - Find the Decoupler module on the one connection holding a part on, ask whether the player's next stage would fire it, and fire it directly with Decoupler.SetIsActive so the split goes through KSA's input buffer on the next frame
* [Set off KSA's own explosion at an arbitrary point, anchored on the planet](ksa-explosions-from-a-point.md) - A burst in mid-air with no vehicle to host it calls ExplosionSystem.SpawnPreset with a BubbleOrigin fixed in the body's rotating frame, real ambient pressure for the pressure-gated stages, and a preset chosen by fireball size because KSA floors explosion intensity
* [Shell holes held in the struck sub-part's frame and painted from depth](bullet-hole-decals.md) - Shell hits found with Part.RayCastEgo are stored in the struck sub-part's own frame and carried to the camera through the matrix the engine draws that sub-part with, and the newest 400 are painted as procedural holes and soot by one tile-gathered compute dispatch before bloom
* [Spatialised gun and rocket-motor sounds driven by held FMOD channels](spatial-gun-and-motor-sounds.md) - Gun fire is an XML CompoundSound (spin-up, loop, tail) played once per firing system and moved every frame, a slow gun gets one pitched gunshot per round, and each burning mod-simulated rocket gets Core's engine loop with its Throttle parameter pinned open
* [Spawn test drones and teleport craft from the panel, a click or a script](spawn-and-teleport-test-craft.md) - Test drones are built from a stock or library craft save on an orbit solved backwards from the requested pass, created under the engine's shapes-registry lock and added to the parent body's children, while craft are moved by queuing the engine's own TeleportToLocation
* [StarMap entry class with per-hook fault containment](starmap-entry-and-fault-containment.md) - One [StarMapMod] class binds six StarMap hooks, wraps every frame hook in its own try/catch and switches the whole mod off after ten faults instead of letting an exception escape into KSA's frame loop
* [Targeting rings painted on the depth buffer](depth-painted-ground-rings.md) - Sight, reach, designation and ICBM target rings are collected in the UI pass anchored to their body and painted before bloom by one compute dispatch per ring that rebuilds the scene point from depth and draws an exact circle or ellipse with a fixed pixel-width line
* [Terrain ground-impact test against the engine's height field](terrain-ground-impact-test.md) - Rounds, sights and cursor rays find the ground through Celestial.GetTerrainHeightFromDirCce(accurate true) clamped to the sea, answered as a centre and a surface radius so a round can test its height at every sub-step for the cost of a subtraction
* [The blast front drawn as a bend in the light, by copying the scene and writing it back at an offset](shock-front-refraction.md) - Each nuclear blast front is drawn as a refracting shell, not an object, by two compute dispatches before bloom that copy KSA's HDR scene image and write each pixel back from the copy, displaced where the view ray grazes the front's sphere
* [The burst's bang, played when its blast front reaches the listener](burst-sound-at-blast-arrival.md) - The nuclear preset ships without Core's SoundEvent, and the mod follows each burst's blast front out to the audio camera, then plays Core's ExplosionBig itself, pitched by yield, as loud as the pressure there, with coincident bursts merged
* [True proportional navigation for mod-flown missiles](proportional-navigation-guidance.md) - Guided rounds steer by true proportional navigation on a back-dated target sample, with gravity biased out, the command projected square to the airflow and clipped to an airframe g limit, and boost or sustain stages pushing along the velocity gained since release
* [TV and thermal look for a camera window, recorded before bloom](sensor-look-pass.md) - A two-dispatch compute pass recorded from the SunbloomRenderer.Render prefix turns one secondary viewport's HDR scene into a TV or white-hot/black-hot picture mapped around its log-mean brightness, measured first into a 1x1 image, so bloom and the tonemap expose it normally
* [Weapon parts as inert KSA parts split into an Assets file and a GameData file](weapon-part-definitions-xml.md) - Each weapon is a plain KSA part declared in two mod.toml-listed XML bundles (art and subparts in KSArmoryAssets.xml, mass, colliders, connectors, editor tags and real RCS thrusters in KSArmoryGameData.xml), with no behaviour in XML because the C# finds the part by Id
* [What a high or airless burst lights in the sky, chosen by the air at the burst](high-altitude-burst-sky.md) - The air density at a nuclear burst picks which sky effects it starts, each drawn as an analytic full-screen compute dispatch tagged by a negative bound in the cloud pass, and an airless body gets one-shot KSA particle emitters instead
* [World-anchored overlays through KSA's gizmo renderer](gizmo-world-overlays.md) - Radar cones, track markers, round trails, shell streaks and placement markers drawn as GizmosRenderer spheres and lines from the StarMap GUI postfix, anchored to the engine's own render position for the platform
* [World-anchored screen markers on full-screen ImGui overlay windows](world-anchored-screen-markers.md) - Brackets, lock cues, chase brackets and part-health bars are projected through the viewport camera's EclToScreen/EgoToScreen onto transparent, input-less, full-screen ImGui windows, with off-screen targets clamped to the screen edge from the camera basis, not the projection
