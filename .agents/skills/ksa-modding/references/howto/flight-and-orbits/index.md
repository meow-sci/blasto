# Subdirectories

* [autopilot](autopilot/index.md) - Flying a craft from code: maneuver nodes, executing burns, the flight computer and attitude modes, per-vehicle autopilots and navigation targets.
* [flight-state](flight-state/index.md) - Reading where a craft is and how it flies: coordinate frames and epochs, flight state, horizon-frame angles, orbit elements, mission time and simulation speed.
* [guidance](guidance/index.md) - Guided flight: suicide burns, powered landing, closed-loop ascent, boostback and glide, ballistic prediction, homing guidance and running heavy solves off the frame.
* [map-view](map-view/index.md) - Map-view interaction: closest approach, points anchored to bodies, and predicted trajectories, markers and orbit lines.
* [transfers](transfers/index.md) - Transfer planning: patched-conic delta-v, multi-leg routes, transfer and launch windows, flyby targeting and extending the stock Transfer Planner.
