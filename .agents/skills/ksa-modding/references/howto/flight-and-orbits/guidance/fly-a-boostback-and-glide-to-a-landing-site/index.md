# Game Feature

* [Fly a boostback burn and glide a booster to its landing site](overview.md) - Fly a boostback burn and then steer an unpowered descent onto a landing site with angle of attack

# How-To

* [Adopt a separated booster and fly a boostback burn from impact predictions](fly-a-boostback-and-glide-to-a-landing-site-1.md) - Record what a separation will throw overboard, adopt the new vehicle as the booster, fly a settle, turn and receding-horizon boostback burn that moves the predicted impact onto the site, and hand over to the glide
* [Glide a booster onto its site with a zero-effort-miss law and an inverted lift curve](fly-a-boostback-and-glide-to-a-landing-site-2.md) - Steer the unpowered fall by tilting the engine end off the airflow, using the impact Jacobian to size the wanted lift and the measured lift curve to turn it into an angle of attack
